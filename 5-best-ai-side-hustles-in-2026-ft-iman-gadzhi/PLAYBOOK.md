### Unboxing speedrun (Seedance 2.5)
SCENE CONTEXT: A vertical comedic unboxing-speedrun UGC video, filmed entirely on the man’s own phone in one continuous recording. A young Black man with a short tight fade, a small gold stud earring, no hat — the man from @Image 1 — is in his kitchen in daylight, wearing a plain white t-shirt and wide black trousers. On the kitchen table: a plain matte black cardboard mailer box sealed with plain black tape, containing the black sporty jersey from @Image 2 — smooth black polyester tricot, lime accents, a wavy lime HIGGS wordmark across the chest reading correctly, 001 patch on the right sleeve, HIGGS and 54 on the back. The chest carries ONLY the wordmark — no badge, no crest. The box and tissue paper are plain with no lettering. Nothing in the scene carries lettering or logos except the jersey. THE CAMERA IS HIS PHONE — the recording device itself — so no phone is ever visible in frame at any moment.

ACTIVE REFERENCES: @Image 1 is the exact face and build of the speaker. @Image 2 is the exact garment in the box — match its graphics exactly and add nothing.

FIRST FRAME: Handheld selfie POV — his face fills the frame close-up, the phone in his extended hand, natural handheld sway, kitchen behind him, he is already talking to the lens with a dead-serious game face.

FORMAT MODE: single continuous take, NO cuts — one unbroken recording from handheld selfie to set-down to the finish; the unbroken take is the whole point of the speedrun.

CAMERA: the camera IS his phone for the entire video, in one continuous move: PHASE 1 — handheld selfie in his extended hand, small natural sway, his face close; PHASE 2 — he lowers the phone and props it upright on the table facing him: the frame swings down with his hand, brief motion blur, a soft thud as the phone lands, the image bounces once and SETTLES into a fixed medium shot — him behind the table, the box in the lower half of frame; PHASE 3 — locked-off for the rest of the video, all speed comes from him. No phone visible in frame at any point, because the phone is the camera.

ACTION AND DIALOGUE: PHASE 1, selfie in hand, dead-serious: "Unboxing speedrun. World record attempt." PHASE 2 — he lowers and plants the phone, steps back into the settled frame, hands hovering over the box like a sprinter at the blocks: "Three. Two. One." PHASE 3 — EXPLOSION of motion, physically continuous: he rips the tape in one pull, flips the lid off — it spins away off-table — yanks the tissue paper out in a cloud, pulls the jersey free, shakes it open with a snap of fabric, dives into it pulling it over his head ON TOP of his white tee, punches his arms through the sleeves, tugs the hem down — and freezes in a finish pose, arms spread, breathing hard, the wavy HIGGS wordmark front and centre, tissue paper still floating down. Beat. He looks at his bare wrist as if checking a stopwatch: "New personal best." Glances down at the jersey, then dead into the lens: "And a new favourite shirt." All dialogue lip-synced, natural pace, American English — the countdown deadly serious, the last two lines calm and deadpan, no announcer voice.

ACTING TASK: Direction — he treats a cardboard box like an Olympic event and never acknowledges the absurdity. Motive — the bit only works if he fully commits. Goal — make the viewer rewatch the rip-to-wear sequence. Obstacle — one smile during the run kills it; only the eyes are allowed to celebrate at the finish. Tactic — sprinter focus, explosive middle, statue-still finish, deadpan verdict at the bare wrist. Eyes: into the lens for the selfie opening and countdown, on the box during the run, back to the lens for the verdict.

PHYSICS: STRICT SOLID-OBJECT CONTACT — the camera motion in PHASE 2 matches a real hand lowering and planting a phone, one bounce, then perfectly still; the tape tears with real resistance, the lid spins off with real momentum, tissue paper crumples and floats naturally, the jersey stretches and settles correctly over his head and shoulders, the hem tug creates real folds; his hands never pass through fabric or cardboard; the table stays stable.

LIGHTING: soft daylight through a kitchen window, warm domestic tones, no ring light.

AUDIO: PHASE 1 voice close to the mic with handheld rustle; the thud and scrape of the phone landing in PHASE 2; then the sharp tape RIP, cardboard thud, tissue rustle, the fabric snap, heavy breathing at the finish; faint room tone, no music — the sound design of the run IS the music.

STYLE: authentic phone-shot UGC, real skin and fabric texture, mild compression, vertical 9:16.

POSITIVE LOCKS: the man’s face and earring match @Image 1 in every frame, no hat; the jersey matches @Image 2 exactly — wavy lime HIGGS wordmark reading correctly once worn, chest carries only the wordmark, no invented badges, lime accents on black, 001 sleeve patch; the box and tissue are plain with zero lettering; ONE continuous take, no cuts, camera = his phone throughout — handheld selfie, then planted on the table, then static; NO phone ever visible in frame; hands never clip through objects; the only lettering in the scene is on the jersey; all speech lip-synced.

## Step-by-Step Recreation Playbook
1. Connect Higgsfield MCP in Claude as shown in Skill Installation.
2. Upload the three skill .md files to Claude Skills.
3. For e-commerce UGC: attach product photo, send the UGC starter prompt. Review outputs. Use full Seedance prompts for specific styles if needed.
4. For motion ads: upload product photo, send the motion prompt. Approve storyboard before final render.
5. For client acquisition: run the client-finder skill. Approve list. Send from connected Gmail. Check local cold-email rules.
6. For real estate: upload client photo, apply the Seedream upgrade prompt from the page. Sell photos + free tour.
7. For websites: prompt Claude with Higgsfield connected to generate images and embed videos.
8. For faceless YouTube: analyze top videos in niche, generate script + thumbnails with GPT Image prompts from the page.
9. For digital products: use the single prompt on the page to research, layout, and generate images + store page.
10. Price packages ($150–$300 entry, retainers later). Deliver same day. Upsell monthly.
